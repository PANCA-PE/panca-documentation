## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Panca?">
    Panca is an all-in-one cloud platform built for restaurants and retail stores in Peru. It brings together a point-of-sale (POS), inventory management, SUNAT electronic invoicing, and a kitchen display system (KDS) in a single system — no spreadsheets, no loose papers, and no end-of-day surprises.

    <Tip>
      Panca runs 100% in your browser. There is nothing to install, and you can access it from any device at any time.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What types of businesses does Panca support?">
    Panca is designed for restaurants and retail stores across Peru, including:

    **Restaurants & food service:** pollerías, cevicherías, cafeterías, chifas, pizzerías, sangucherías, marisquerías, fast food, dark kitchens, and bars.

    **Retail stores:** bodegas, minimarkets, ferreterías, farmacias, tiendas de ropa y calzado, librerías, licorerías, distribuidoras, veterinarias, ópticas, and more.

    If your business sells products or serves food and needs inventory control and SUNAT invoicing, Panca works for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Panca work on my phone or tablet?">
    Yes. Panca is 100% web-based, so it runs on any device with a modern browser — smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer. You don't need to install any app or software. Your team can start using it in minutes without any technical knowledge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Panca work without internet?">
    Yes. Panca includes an offline mode that lets you keep selling even when your internet connection drops. Your sales data syncs automatically once connectivity is restored, so you never lose a transaction.

    <Note>
      Offline mode is included in all plans — Básico, Profesional, and Empresarial.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## SUNAT & Invoicing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Panca SUNAT-homologated?">
    Yes. Panca operates as an authorized Electronic Service Provider (PSE — Proveedor de Servicios Electrónicos) homologated by SUNAT. Every boleta, factura, and nota de crédito you issue through Panca is 100% valid under Peruvian tax law. You comply with the normativa tributaria automatically, with no extra effort.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to start invoicing electronically?">
    You need two things:

    1. **Your RUC** — your business's tax identification number registered with SUNAT.
    2. **Your clave SOL** — the password you use to access SUNAT's online portal (SUNAT Operaciones en Línea).

    The Panca onboarding team activates your SUNAT connection for you during setup at no additional cost.

    <Note>
      If you don't yet have a clave SOL, you can obtain one directly from any SUNAT office or through their online portal at sunat.gob.pe.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if SUNAT is temporarily unavailable?">
    Panca includes an automatic retry system. If SUNAT's servers are down or experiencing delays, Panca queues your comprobantes and resends them automatically once the connection is restored. Your sales are never interrupted, and all documents are validated as soon as SUNAT is back online.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long are my invoicing documents stored?">
    Panca stores all your XML files and CDR (Constancia de Recepción) acknowledgement records for **5 years** in the cloud. You can access and download them at any time from your dashboard at no additional cost — no manual archiving required on your end.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setup & Hardware

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    You can start your first sale in under **5 minutes** — just create your account and load your menu or product catalog. Most businesses are fully operational the same day.

    For a complete setup — including inventory configuration, SUNAT connection activation, and team training — the Panca onboarding team runs a guided session that typically takes **30–60 minutes**. This service is included in every plan at no extra cost.

    <Tip>
      The Panca team can load your catalog and recipes for you. Just send them your menu or product list and they'll handle the rest.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need special hardware to use Panca?">
    No. Since Panca runs entirely in the browser, any device with an internet connection works — phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop.

    Panca is also fully compatible with the following peripheral hardware if you choose to use it:

    - **Thermal receipt printers** — for printing tickets and comandas
    - **Barcode scanners** — USB or Bluetooth, for fast product lookup
    - **Cash drawers** — for cash management and automatic end-of-day reconciliation

    None of these are required to get started. You can add them at any time as your operation grows.

    <Note>
      Hardware configuration is handled remotely by the Panca support team for all peripherals. Kiosk setups require an on-site visit from the Panca team.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import my existing products from Excel?">
    Yes. Panca provides a ready-to-use Excel template. Fill in your product names, prices, categories, and barcodes, then upload the file — your entire catalog loads in seconds. This works for both restaurant menus and retail product inventories with hundreds or thousands of SKUs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Inventory

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Panca track my inventory automatically?">
    Yes, in the **Profesional plan**. Every time you make a sale, Panca automatically deducts the corresponding ingredients or products from your stock based on your configured recipes or product units. You always see your real inventory levels in real time without counting manually.

    You also receive automatic low-stock alerts so you can reorder before you run out of your best-selling items.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I track lot numbers and expiry dates?">
    Yes. Panca supports lot-number tracking and expiry-date management for businesses that sell perishable or regulated products. The system alerts you before a product expires so you can take action early.

    This is especially useful for **farmacias and boticas** (pharmaceutical lot and expiry compliance) and **bodegas and minimarkets** (perishable goods rotation).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data & Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Your data always belongs to you. If you decide to leave Panca, you can export a complete copy of all your sales history, products, customer records, and inventory data to Excel at any time — at no cost and with no restrictions. There is no data lock-in.

    <Warning>
      Export your data before your subscription ends to ensure you have a local copy. Panca recommends doing this as a regular backup practice regardless of whether you plan to cancel.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is my data stored and how reliable is the platform?">
    Panca is a cloud-based platform with **99.9% uptime**. Your data is stored securely in the cloud, backed up automatically, and accessible from any device at any time. You don't need to manage servers, perform manual backups, or worry about hardware failures taking your data with them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
