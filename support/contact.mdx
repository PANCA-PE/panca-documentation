## What the Panca Team Does for You

Support at Panca goes well beyond answering questions. When you sign up, the team handles the entire onboarding process so you can focus on running your business from day one.

### Initial Setup & Catalog Loading

You don't have to configure Panca on your own. Send the Panca team your menu, product list, or an Excel file with your catalog and they load everything into your account for you — products, prices, categories, and recipes. Most businesses are fully operational the same day they sign up.

### SUNAT Connection Activation

The Panca team activates your electronic invoicing connection with SUNAT during onboarding. You provide your RUC and clave SOL, and they handle the technical configuration so your boletas and facturas start flowing from the very first sale.

### Hardware Configuration

If you use thermal printers, barcode scanners, or a cash drawer, the Panca team configures them remotely — no need for an in-person visit or a technician. The only exception is kiosk setups, which require an on-site installation appointment coordinated with the Panca team.

### Ongoing Support for Any Issue

Once you're up and running, the Panca team stays available for anything that comes up: a question about a report, a change to your menu, a SUNAT document that needs attention, or help adding a new user. There is no limit on support requests and no extra charge for ongoing assistance.
