Getting up and running with Panca is fast by design. You create your account, hand off your menu or product list, and the Panca team does the rest — catalog loading, SUNAT activation, and a live onboarding walkthrough with your team. Most businesses are taking real orders on the same day they sign up.

## What You Need Before You Start

- A valid email address
- Your RUC (for SUNAT-linked invoicing)
- Your SUNAT clave SOL *(only needed for activating electronic invoicing — the Panca team will request this during setup)*
- Your menu or product catalog *(a photo, PDF, or Excel file is fine)*

<Note>
  Your RUC and clave SOL are required to issue SUNAT-compliant boletas and facturas. If you don't have your clave SOL handy, you can still create your account and start exploring Panca — just complete the SUNAT connection before your first real sale.
</Note>

## Go Live in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account (2 minutes)">
    Go to [panca.pe](https://www.panca.pe) and click **Agendar demostración** or start your free account directly. Enter your email and RUC — no credit card required, no software to install. Panca is 100% web, so it opens immediately in your browser on any device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Panca configures your catalog (no work from you)">
    Send the Panca team your menu or product list — a WhatsApp photo, a PDF, or an Excel file all work. The team loads your items, prices, and categories into your account for you. You don't need to type a single product manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Panca activates your SUNAT connection (no work from you)">
    Provide your RUC and clave SOL to your onboarding specialist and the team connects your account to SUNAT. From this point on, every sale automatically generates a valid boleta or factura electrónica — sent to SUNAT in real time and stored in your account for five years.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start selling — same day">
    Open Panca on your tablet, phone, or PC. Take your first order, collect payment (cash, card, Yape, or Plin), and issue your first electronic receipt. You're live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Onboarding Session Covers

After your account is configured, a Panca specialist walks your team through a 30–60 minute onboarding session at no extra cost. The session covers:

- Navigating the POS and taking your first order
- Managing tables or the sales counter, depending on your business type
- Printing comandas or sending orders to the kitchen display
- Issuing boletas and facturas from the checkout screen
- Reading your end-of-day sales report and closing the register

Your team doesn't need any prior experience with POS software. The interface is built to be learned in minutes.

## Offline Mode

If your internet connection drops, Panca keeps working. You can continue taking orders and registering sales in offline mode. When your connection is restored, all data syncs automatically. You never lose a transaction.
