Panca Inventory gives you a live view of everything in your warehouse — ingredients, products, and supplies — without manual counting or spreadsheets. Every time you complete a sale, Panca deducts the right quantities from stock automatically, whether that's based on a recipe for a restaurant dish or a product unit for a retail store. The result: your numbers are always accurate, your team is always prepared, and customers consistently report **up to 30% less waste** after switching to Panca.

<Note>
  Inventory management is available on the **Profesional** and **Empresarial** plans. Features such as multi-warehouse transfers require the Empresarial plan.
</Note>

## How inventory works

Panca connects your sales to your stock in real time. Here's the end-to-end flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load your ingredients or products">
    Add every item you stock — ingredients for a restaurant or SKUs for a store. Set the name, unit of measure (kg, liters, units), and starting stock level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link sales to stock">
    For restaurants, attach a recipe (ficha técnica) to each menu item so Panca knows exactly which ingredients to deduct per sale. For retail stores, each product unit is decremented directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell — stock updates automatically">
    Every completed sale in the POS triggers an instant stock deduction. No manual entry, no end-of-day adjustments, no surprises.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor, get alerted, and reorder">
    Your inventory dashboard shows live stock levels, flags products below their minimum threshold, and warns you about items approaching their expiry date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who inventory is for

Panca Inventory is designed for both food service and retail businesses in Peru:

- **Restaurants, pollerías, cafeterías, and cevicherías** — track ingredients by weight and volume, calculate food cost per dish, and eliminate recipe guesswork.
- **Bodegas, minimarkets, and pharmacies** — manage hundreds or thousands of SKUs, track lot numbers and expiry dates, and scan products with a barcode reader.
- **Multi-branch operations** — centralize stock across all locations and transfer inventory between warehouses (Empresarial plan).
