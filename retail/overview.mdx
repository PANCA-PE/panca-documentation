PANCA Negocios is a 100% web-based platform that brings together your point of sale, inventory control, SUNAT electronic invoicing, and business reports in a single system. You access it from any device — phone, tablet, or computer — with no software to install and no per-sale commissions to worry about. Whether you run a corner bodega, a growing minimarket, a ferretería with thousands of SKUs, or a botica with lot-tracking requirements, Panca fits your operation from day one. Most stores are up and selling in under 24 hours, with your product catalog imported directly from Excel.

## Key differentiators

- **S/119/mes, no commissions.** One fixed monthly fee covers unlimited users, all modules, and 24/7 priority support. You pay the same whether you sell S/1,000 or S/100,000 a month.
- **Same-day setup.** The Panca team loads your product catalog, connects your SUNAT credentials, and trains your staff — most stores are fully operational the same day.
- **Import from Excel.** Upload your entire product list — codes, prices, stock levels — from a spreadsheet in seconds using the provided template.
- **100% web, no installs.** Run Panca from a browser on any device. Add cashier stations without buying new licenses.
- **Multi-branch ready.** The Empresarial plan centralizes inventory, transfers, and reporting across all your locations from one dashboard.
