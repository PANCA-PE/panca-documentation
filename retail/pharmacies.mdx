Running a farmacia or botica in Peru means managing hundreds or thousands of medications, each with its own lot number and expiry date. A product that expires on your shelf is pure loss — and in a regulated environment, selling near-expiry stock without a system creates compliance risk. PANCA Negocios gives your pharmacy complete lot-level inventory control: you register each lot with its expiry date when stock arrives, the system guides you to sell the oldest lots first (FEFO), alerts you before anything expires, and flags medications that are running low. All of this integrates directly with your point of sale and SUNAT electronic invoicing so your team works from one screen, not three.

## How the core pharmacy workflows work

### Lot tracking {#lot-tracking}

When a new shipment arrives, you create a goods receipt in Panca and enter each product's lot number and expiry date. From that point on, Panca tracks that lot as a distinct unit of inventory — separately from other lots of the same medication. You always know exactly how many units of each lot you have and when they expire.

### FEFO: sell oldest stock first {#fefo}

<Warning>
  **FEFO compliance protects your customers and your business.** Selling medications past their expiry date is a health risk and a regulatory violation in Peru. Panca's FEFO logic automatically surfaces the oldest lot at the point of sale so your staff always dispenses the right product — without needing to memorize which lot to reach for.
</Warning>

When a cashier scans a medication barcode, Panca selects the lot with the nearest expiry date to deduct from first. This happens automatically — your team doesn't need to sort or check shelves manually. If a lot is approaching expiry but hasn't sold down yet, Panca raises an alert so you can take action: a promotion, a return to your distributor, or a transfer to a higher-volume location.

### Minimum stock alerts {#stock-alerts}

You configure a minimum stock level for each medication in your catalog. When total available units across all active lots falls below that threshold, Panca sends an alert. This is especially important for high-demand or narrow-margin medications where a stockout means lost sales and frustrated customers.

### Fast search by name or active ingredient

Your team can search the product catalog by trade name, active ingredient (principio activo), brand, or barcode. This matters when a customer asks for a generic equivalent or when a medication is known by multiple names in your market. Results appear instantly even across catalogs of thousands of products.

## Getting started with lot tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add medications with lot numbers and expiry dates">
    Create your product catalog — you can import from Excel to load hundreds of medications at once. For each product, enter its lot number and expiry date when you receive new stock. Panca's guided goods receipt form makes this fast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure minimum stock levels per medication">
    For each product, set the minimum quantity below which you want to be alerted. Start with your fastest-moving and most critical medications. You can adjust these thresholds at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Panca alert you before expiry and low stock">
    Panca monitors your inventory continuously. When a lot approaches expiry or a product drops below its minimum level, you receive an automatic alert — so you can act before the situation becomes a problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FEFO guides your daily sales automatically">
    From the first sale, Panca selects the correct lot at checkout without requiring any action from your staff. The oldest expiry date is always deducted first, keeping your inventory compliant and your customers safe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Lot tracking and FEFO are available in the Profesional plan (S/119/mes).** This plan also includes unlimited users, multiple price lists, margin reports, and 24/7 priority support. The Básico plan (S/99/mes) covers POS, barcode scanning, and SUNAT invoicing. Multi-branch control across several botica locations requires the Empresarial plan — contact Panca to get a custom quote based on the number of locations you operate.
</Note>

## SUNAT electronic invoicing for pharmacies

Every sale generates a boleta or factura validated by SUNAT in seconds. This is important for customers who purchase on behalf of insurance plans or companies and need a valid factura. Notas de crédito for returns and adjustments are also handled directly in Panca with a guided step-by-step flow. Your accountant gets a monthly export with all transactions and IGV already calculated.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Does Panca handle medications with multiple active lots at the same time?">
  Yes. Each lot is tracked independently. When you receive a new shipment of the same medication with a different lot number, Panca adds it as a separate lot entry. The FEFO logic automatically sequences sales across all active lots — oldest first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I manage convention prices for EPS clients?">
  Yes. The Profesional plan supports multiple price lists. You configure each list (for example, regular, EPS convenio, corporate) and select the appropriate one at the point of sale for each transaction.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Panca work for independent boticas as well as chains?">
  Yes. A single-location botica uses the Profesional plan at S/119/mes for full lot tracking and FEFO. A chain of boticas uses the Empresarial plan for centralized multi-location control. Both use exactly the same lot tracking and FEFO functionality.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does setup take for a pharmacy?">
  Most boticas are fully operational within 24 hours. The Panca team helps you import your product catalog, configure lot tracking, set up SUNAT invoicing, and train your staff — at no additional cost.
</Accordion>
