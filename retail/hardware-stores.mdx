## How Panca handles large ferretería catalogs

### Fast search across thousands of products {#catalog}

Your team can find any product in seconds by searching on the barcode, SKU code, product name, category, or brand. This is critical when a customer asks for a specific bolt dimension or a particular brand of electrical conduit and your cashier needs an instant answer — not a ten-minute search through drawers and lists.

Every product gets a unique identifier. If you use your own internal SKU codes in addition to manufacturer barcodes, Panca supports both simultaneously.

### Wholesale and retail price lists {#price-lists}

<Accordion title="How automatic price list selection works">
  You configure two or more price lists — for example, **Precio Minorista** and **Precio Mayorista**. Each customer in your system is assigned to a default list. When a cashier opens a sale and selects the customer (or customer type), Panca loads the correct prices automatically. There are no manual adjustments and no risk of accidentally giving a retail customer a wholesale price or vice versa.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Adding more price tiers">
  You can create as many price lists as your business needs — for example, a contractor tier, a government-project tier, or a VIP customer tier. Each list sets different prices per product or applies a blanket discount percentage to your base prices.
</Accordion>

### Multi-warehouse inventory with inter-branch transfers {#multi-warehouse}

<Accordion title="Tracking stock across multiple locations">
  Each branch or warehouse has its own inventory ledger in Panca. When you sell from a location, that location's stock deducts. When you receive merchandise at a warehouse, that warehouse's stock increases. You always know what each location holds without calling anyone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Processing inter-branch transfers">
  When one location runs low on a product that another location has in abundance, you initiate a transfer directly in Panca. The sending warehouse's stock decreases and the receiving warehouse's stock increases once the transfer is confirmed — creating a clean audit trail with no manual reconciliation.
</Accordion>

### Margin reports by product and category {#reports}

<Tip>
  **Use category-level margin reports to find your most profitable lines.** Ferreterías often discover that a high-volume category like tornillos operates on thin margins, while specialty eléctricos or premium herramientas carry much higher margins on lower volume. Panca's category reports make this visible so you can adjust your purchasing mix and shelf allocation accordingly.
</Tip>

Panca calculates the margin on every product based on the cost you registered versus the price you sold it at. You can view this by individual product, by category (tornillos, herramientas, pintura, eléctricos), by supplier, or across the whole store — for any time period you choose. Access these reports from your phone at any time without being in the store.

### SUNAT facturas for construction clients and businesses

Construction companies, contractors, and business clients almost always require a factura — not a boleta — to claim IGV credit. Panca issues both boletas and facturas validated by SUNAT in seconds at the point of sale. Notas de crédito for returns or corrections follow a simple guided flow. Your accountant receives a monthly export with all transactions and IGV already calculated.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your full product catalog from Excel">
    Download Panca's product import template, fill in your SKU codes, product names, categories, barcodes, costs, and prices, then upload the file. Ferreterías with 2,000–5,000 products typically complete this step in under an hour. The Panca team can handle the import for you if you prefer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up wholesale and retail price lists">
    In the pricing settings, create your price lists and assign prices per product (or set percentage rules). Then assign each customer or customer type to their default list. From that point on, prices apply automatically at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure minimum stock levels per product">
    Set reorder thresholds for your fastest-moving products and any items where a stockout would stop a job site. Panca alerts you when inventory drops below those levels so you reorder before you run out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start selling with barcodes">
    Connect your USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner. Your team scans, Panca pulls the product and its price, the cashier selects customer type, and the sale is done. Stock updates automatically and SUNAT receives the comprobante in seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
