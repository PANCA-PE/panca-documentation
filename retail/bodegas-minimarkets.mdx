## Bodegas: built for hundreds of products

Panca handles everything from abarrotes and golosinas to cleaning products and cold drinks — all identified by barcode. You scan, Panca charges, and your stock updates automatically.

### Key features for bodegas

<Accordion title="Barcode scanning with any USB or Bluetooth reader">
  Connect any standard barcode scanner and start ringing up products immediately. Your team learns the checkout flow in minutes — it's as simple as scanning and selecting a payment method.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Automatic stock deduction per sale">
  Every product sold deducts exactly the right quantity from your inventory without you touching a spreadsheet. You always know what you have in real time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Expiry date tracking and alerts">
  Register expiry dates when you receive merchandise. Panca alerts you before products expire so you can put them on promotion or return them to your supplier before they become a loss.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Yape, Plin, card, and cash — all registered automatically">
  Accept any payment method and Panca records it against the sale. Your end-of-day cash reconciliation happens in one click.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Real-time profit from your phone">
  Check today's sales, your best-selling products, and your profit margins from your phone at any time — whether you're at the store or not.
</Accordion>

## Minimarkets: compete with the big chains

Minimarkets face the same technology that Tambo+ and Oxxo use every day. Panca puts those same tools — fast multi-register POS, real-time inventory, centralized multi-branch control, and intelligent reports — in your hands at a fraction of the cost.

### Key features for minimarkets

<Accordion title="Fast POS built for rush hour (hora punta)">
  Multiple cashier stations can operate simultaneously on any device. All sales sync in real time, inventory updates automatically, and queues keep moving even during your busiest hours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Multi-branch centralized stock control">
  If you operate more than one minimarket location, Panca's Empresarial plan gives you a single dashboard to view and manage inventory across all stores. No more calling each location to check what they have.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Smart reports: peak hours and category margins">
  See exactly which hours drive the most revenue so you can staff accordingly. Margin reports by product category show you where your profit really comes from — so you stock smarter, not just more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Minimum stock alerts — never run out of your top sellers">
  Set a minimum stock level for each product. Panca notifies you when stock drops below that threshold so you reorder before you run out and lose sales.
</Accordion>

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at [negocios.panca.pe](https://negocios.panca.pe/register) in under two minutes. No credit card required. You get a 3-day free trial with full access to all features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your products from Excel">
    Download Panca's product template, fill in your product names, barcodes, prices, costs, and stock quantities, then upload the file. Your entire catalog loads in seconds. The Panca team can also do this step for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect SUNAT invoicing">
    Panca activates your boleta and factura electronic invoicing and connects to SUNAT at no additional charge. From that point on, every sale generates a valid electronic receipt automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start selling">
    Scan your first product, select a payment method, and you're done. Your inventory updates, your receipt goes to SUNAT, and your reports reflect the sale — all in seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Does Panca work for a small bodega with just a few hundred products?">
  Yes. Panca is designed specifically for bodegas of all sizes, from small neighborhood stores to multi-location minimarket chains. The Negocios plan at S/119/mes gives you everything: POS, automatic inventory, SUNAT invoicing, and reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to buy special hardware?">
  No. Panca runs in any web browser — on a computer, tablet, or phone. A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner speeds up checkout but is not required to start. A thermal receipt printer and cash drawer are optional accessories.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I run multiple cash registers at the same time?">
  Yes. Because Panca is web-based, you can open it on as many devices as you need. All sales sync in real time and inventory updates across every station simultaneously. The Negocios plan includes unlimited users.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if my internet goes down?">
  Panca is designed to be resilient. Contact the support team via WhatsApp 24/7 for guidance on contingency workflows. For critical connectivity scenarios, the team can advise on local network configurations.
</Accordion>
