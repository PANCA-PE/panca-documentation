Panca gives you a live window into how your business is performing — no matter where you are. Every sale, every dish, and every shift updates your reports in real time, so you can make decisions based on what's actually happening in your restaurant or store right now, not yesterday's export from a spreadsheet. Whether you're at the register, at home, or on the go, the same dashboard is available from your phone, tablet, or PC.

## What the Dashboard Shows You

When you open **Reports**, you see a consolidated view of your business at a glance. The top bar displays today's total revenue, number of transactions, and average ticket size. Below that, Panca breaks the data into panels you can drill into:

- **Revenue over time** — a chart of sales by hour, day, week, or custom date range, letting you spot trends and slow periods instantly.
- **Top-selling products** — a ranked list of your best performers by units sold and revenue, so you always know what's driving the business.
- **Category breakdown** — sales and margin grouped by menu category or product family, helping you see which areas of your offering are most profitable.
- **Waiter performance** *(restaurants)* — sales, covers, and tips per team member so you can coach your staff with real data.
- **Peak hours analysis** — a heat map of transaction volume by hour and day of week, revealing when to staff up and when to prep more.
- **Food cost per dish** *(Profesional plan, restaurants)* — the actual cost of ingredients versus the selling price for each dish, so you know your true margin at the recipe level.

## Run a Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports from the main menu">
    In the left-hand navigation, click **Reports**. The dashboard loads your current day's data automatically. You'll see the summary cards for revenue, transactions, and average ticket at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your date range">
    Use the date picker in the top-right corner to choose a preset (Today, This Week, This Month, Last Month) or enter a custom start and end date. The entire dashboard refreshes to reflect the period you selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your report type">
    Click any panel header — such as **Sales by Product**, **Category Margins**, **Waiter Performance**, or **Peak Hours** — to expand the full detailed view. You can switch between chart and table layouts using the toggle in the top-right of each panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to Excel or PDF">
    Inside any report, click **Export** and choose your format. Panca generates the file instantly and downloads it to your device — ready to share with your accountant, partners, or management team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Report Types

<Accordion title="Sales by Period">
  View revenue broken down by hour, day, week, or month. Compare any two periods side by side to measure growth or spot seasonal dips. The chart view highlights your highest-revenue days automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Best-Selling Products Ranking">
  A sorted list of every product or dish, ranked by units sold and total revenue. Filter by category to compare items within the same group. Use this to plan purchasing, update your menu, and identify slow-moving items to promote or retire.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Margin and Profitability">
  Panca calculates the gross margin for each product, category, and supplier. You can see at a glance which items contribute most to your bottom line and which are dragging it down. Available at the product, category, and supplier level on all plans.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Waiter Performance and Tips (Restaurants)">
  Each waiter's sales total, number of covers served, average ticket size, and tips received appear in a single table. You can filter by shift or date range and export the results for payroll or performance reviews.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Peak Hours Analysis">
  A heat map shows transaction volume by hour of day and day of week. Green cells are your quietest periods; red cells are your busiest. Use this to decide when to schedule extra staff, start prep earlier, or run a promotion to fill slow slots.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Food Cost per Dish (Profesional Plan, Restaurants)">
  Panca tracks the cost of every ingredient in your recipes and compares it to the selling price of each dish. You see the food cost percentage and gross profit for every item on your menu, updated automatically as ingredient prices change. This report is available on the **Profesional** plan.
</Accordion>

## Advanced Analytics (Profesional Plan)

On the **Profesional** plan, Panca unlocks a deeper layer of analytics beyond the standard reports:

- **Expense tracking** — log operating expenses (rent, utilities, supplies) and see them alongside revenue so you can calculate true net margin.
- **Cost trend analysis** — track how ingredient costs evolve over weeks and months.
- **Dish profitability ranking** — sort your entire menu by profit contribution, not just revenue, to find your hidden stars.
- **Consolidated reporting across periods** — compare this month to the same month last year in a single view.

<Note>
  Food cost per dish and advanced analytics require the **Profesional** plan (S/119/month). You can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Plan** without losing any data.
</Note>

<Tip>
  At the end of your day, open Panca on your phone and check the **Today's Summary** card before you leave. In under a minute you'll know your total revenue, your top-selling item, and whether any product underperformed — so you walk in tomorrow already knowing what to focus on.
</Tip>
