Panca is a 100% cloud-based business management platform built specifically for Peru. Whether you run a cevichería in Miraflores, a bodega in Trujillo, or a multi-branch pollería chain, Panca brings your point of sale, inventory, electronic invoicing, and kitchen operations into one unified system — no installs, no spreadsheets, no end-of-month headaches.

## Who Panca Is For

Panca serves two main business types across Peru:

**Restaurants** — pollerías, cevicherías, cafeterías, chifas, pizzerías, dark kitchens, and any food-service operation that needs to manage tables, send orders to the kitchen, and issue SUNAT-compliant receipts at the point of checkout.

**Retail stores** — bodegas, minimarkets, ferreterías, farmacias, licorerías, tiendas de ropa, and any product-based business that needs fast barcode scanning, accurate inventory tracking, and automatic electronic billing.

## Platform Highlights

Panca is designed so your team can start selling on day one, not day ten. Here is what makes it different from a generic POS:

- **100% web, no installation.** Open a browser on any device and you're ready. Works on tablets, laptops, smartphones, and desktop PCs.
- **Offline mode.** Keep taking orders and sales even when your internet connection drops. Data syncs automatically when you're back online.
- **Multi-device.** Your cashier, your floor manager, and your kitchen all work simultaneously from their own screens on the same account.
- **SUNAT-homologated invoicing.** Boletas and facturas are validated in real time. Automatic retries handle SUNAT downtime so you never block a sale.
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS).** Orders flow from the floor straight to kitchen screens the moment they're placed — no paper tickets, no shouting across the pass. *(Profesional and Empresarial plans.)*
- **No per-sale commissions.** You pay a flat monthly fee regardless of your sales volume.
