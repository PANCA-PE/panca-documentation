Panca offers three plans sized for different stages of your business — from a single-location restaurant or store just getting started to a multi-branch chain that needs deep integrations and a dedicated account team. Every plan includes 24/7 support, SUNAT invoicing, and full access to your data at all times.

<Note>
  All plans are available on a monthly or annual billing cycle. Paying annually saves you the equivalent of two months — the same features, less spend per year.
</Note>

## Choosing the Right Plan

<Tip>
  **Start with Profesional if inventory or food cost matters to your business.** Most growing restaurants and retail stores find that the automatic stock deduction and recipe cost tracking in Profesional pays for itself quickly — the S/20/mes difference from Básico is a small cost compared to the waste it prevents.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  The **Kitchen Display System (KDS)** and **automatic inventory control** are only available on the Profesional and Empresarial plans. If these features are a priority for you, the Básico plan will not include them.
</Warning>

**Choose Básico if:**
- You're digitizing your operations for the first time and want to start simple.
- You have five or fewer staff members using the system.
- Your primary needs are POS, SUNAT invoicing, and comanda printing.

**Choose Profesional if:**
- You want real-time inventory tracking and automatic food-cost calculation.
- You have a kitchen that needs a display system (KDS) instead of printed tickets.
- You need unlimited user accounts for your team.
- You want priority 24/7 support with faster response times.

**Choose Empresarial if:**
- Your monthly revenue exceeds S/70,000.
- You operate more than one branch or plan to expand.
- You need to integrate Panca with delivery platforms (Rappi, PedidosYa) or your accounting software.
- You want a dedicated advisor and a guaranteed SLA for your operation.
