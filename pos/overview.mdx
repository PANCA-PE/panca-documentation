Panca POS is the all-in-one point of sale that runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no dedicated hardware, no IT setup. Whether you run a restaurant with dozens of tables or a retail store with hundreds of SKUs, Panca connects your front-of-house, back-of-house, payments, and SUNAT invoicing in a single system you open on any tablet, phone, laptop, or PC.

## Two modes, one platform

Panca adapts to how your business actually works. Choose the mode that fits your operation, or run both in the same account if you manage multiple business types.

## What's included in every plan

- **Multi-device access** — open Panca on as many tablets, phones, or computers as you need; all data syncs instantly
- **Offline mode** — keep selling even if your internet drops; transactions sync when connectivity returns
- **SUNAT electronic invoicing** — boletas, facturas, and notas de crédito included in all plans, with unlimited comprobantes
- **Cierre de caja** — one-click daily cash close with a full breakdown by payment method
- **No per-sale commissions** — Panca charges a flat monthly fee regardless of your sales volume
