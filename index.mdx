## How to Go Live with Panca

New to Panca? Here's the complete onboarding path from zero to your first sale:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at [panca.pe](https://www.panca.pe) with your email and RUC. No credit card required. No software to install. Takes about two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Panca team loads your catalog">
    Send your menu or product list — a photo, PDF, or Excel file works. The Panca onboarding team loads all your items, prices, and categories into your account for you. You don't type a single product manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Panca team connects SUNAT">
    Provide your RUC and clave SOL to your onboarding specialist. The team activates your electronic invoicing so that every sale automatically generates a SUNAT-valid boleta or factura from that point on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start selling">
    Open Panca on any browser — tablet, phone, or PC. Take your first order, collect payment, and issue your first electronic receipt. You're live, the same day you signed up.
  </Step>
</Steps>
