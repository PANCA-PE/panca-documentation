Panca gives you a live, visual floor plan of your entire dining room so you always know the state of every table at a glance. Color-coded statuses, waiter assignments, and one-tap bill management replace the guesswork and the back-and-forth that slow down busy service. Whether you run a 10-table bistro or a full-service restaurant with more than 100 covers, the floor plan scales with you and keeps your whole team in sync from the moment the first guest sits down.

## Table Status at a Glance

## How to Manage Tables During Service

<Steps>
  <Step title="View the floor plan">
    Open Panca on any device — tablet, phone, or laptop — and your full floor plan loads instantly. You can see every table's current color status, the assigned waiter, and how long each table has been occupied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a table">
    Tap any green (free) table to open it. Select the waiter responsible for that table and confirm the number of guests. The table changes to orange immediately so the rest of the team knows it is occupied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the order">
    The assigned waiter selects items from the digital menu, adds modifiers (e.g., "no onion", "extra spicy"), and sends the order to the kitchen in one tap. The comanda reaches the kitchen screen in seconds — no paper, no shouting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the bill">
    When guests are ready to pay, tap the table and choose from three options:

    - **Split the bill** — divide items between any number of guests.
    - **Merge bills** — combine two tables onto a single check for groups that were seated separately.
    - **Transfer the order** — move all open items to a different table number without losing any order history.

    The table turns red once the bill is generated and payment is pending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the table after payment">
    Accept payment (cash, Yape, Plin, or card) and issue the SUNAT-validated receipt with one tap. Panca marks the table green again automatically, ready for the next guests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  </Card>

<Tip>
  If a group of guests needs to move to a different table mid-meal, use **Order Transfer** instead of manually re-entering the order. Tap the occupied table, select "Transfer order," and choose the destination table. The entire order — including modifiers and partial items already sent to the kitchen — moves over in one step, and the kitchen is notified automatically.
</Tip>
