Panca centralizes every delivery and remote-ordering channel into a single management screen. Whether a customer orders through your own branded online store, scans a QR code at the table, or places an order on Rappi or PedidosYa, the order arrives in Panca automatically and routes straight to your kitchen. You stop juggling multiple tablets and separate apps, your team works from one place, and you never miss an order regardless of where it came from.

<Note>
  Aggregator integrations (Rappi and PedidosYa) and the custom API are available on the **Empresarial** plan. The Online Store and QR Table Ordering channels are available on all plans. [Contact the Panca team](https://wa.me/51998338835) to discuss an Empresarial upgrade.
</Note>

## Channel Deep Dives

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Online Store">
    Your Panca-powered online store gives you a fully branded ordering page that you own. Customers visit your URL, browse your menu with photos and prices, and check out using their preferred payment method. Every order lands in your Panca POS queue in real time, prints to the kitchen, and is tracked just like a dine-in order.

    **Key benefits:**
    - Zero commission to any third-party platform
    - Menu updates in Panca reflect on the store instantly
    - Full order history and sales data in your Panca dashboard
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="QR Table Ordering">
    Place a printed QR code on each table. When customers scan it, they see your full digital menu on their smartphone, select items, and confirm the order — all without waiting for a waiter. The order appears on the KDS in the kitchen immediately.

    **Key benefits:**
    - Reduces wait time during peak hours
    - Frees up waiters to focus on service rather than order-taking
    - Works on any smartphone, no app download required
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PedidosYa">
    Connect your Panca account to your PedidosYa restaurant profile. Once linked, every incoming PedidosYa order is injected directly into your Panca order queue. There is no need to watch a separate PedidosYa tablet or manually retype orders into your POS.

    **Key benefits:**
    - Eliminates double-entry errors
    - Kitchen receives PedidosYa orders at the same time as dine-in orders
    - Requires **Empresarial** plan
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rappi">
    Link your Rappi restaurant account to Panca and incoming Rappi orders appear in your unified order screen automatically. Your kitchen staff sees them in the same queue as every other channel, so preparation times stay consistent and nothing gets missed.

    **Key benefits:**
    - One screen for all channels — no more tablet per platform
    - Order status updates sync back to the Rappi platform
    - Requires **Empresarial** plan
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom API">
    The Panca API lets you connect any custom delivery channel, ordering kiosk, or third-party platform to your Panca account. Use it to build your own WhatsApp ordering bot, integrate a loyalty app, or connect a local delivery provider.

    **Key benefits:**
    - Full flexibility to integrate any channel you operate
    - Orders from custom sources follow the same routing as all other channels
    - Requires **Empresarial** plan; dedicated onboarding support included
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

  Before activating Rappi and PedidosYa at the same time, calculate the net margin per channel after commissions. Delivery apps typically charge between 25% and 35% per order. Panca's cross-channel sales report makes it easy to compare profitability so you can decide where to focus your promotion budget.
</Tip>
