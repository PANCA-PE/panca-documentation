The Panca QR digital menu (Carta QR) lets your customers browse your full menu on their own smartphone by scanning a single QR code you place on each table. Every item you have set up in Panca — complete with photos, descriptions, prices, and variants — appears on their screen in seconds. When you change a price, add a new dish, or mark something as sold out, the update goes live instantly for every customer in the room. No reprinting, no sticker corrections, no waiting on a designer.

<Note>
  The Carta QR is included on the **Básico**, **Profesional**, and **Empresarial** plans — no add-on required. Every Panca account comes ready to generate and display your QR menu from day one.
</Note>

## How Customers Use the Carta QR

When a customer sits down, they scan the QR code on the table with their smartphone camera. No app download is required. Their browser opens your digital menu instantly, showing your full catalogue with photos and prices organized by category. Depending on your plan and configuration, customers can either browse and tell their waiter what they want, or place and confirm their order directly from the menu — in which case the order goes straight to the kitchen.

## Setting Up Your Digital Menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your menu in Panca with photos and prices">
    In the Panca back office, create your menu categories (starters, mains, desserts, drinks) and add each item with its name, description, price, and a high-quality photo. For each item, you can also define variants — different sizes, preparations, or add-ons (see below). Everything you add here powers both the physical POS and the QR menu simultaneously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your QR code">
    Once your menu is configured, go to **Settings → Carta QR** and download the QR code image. Panca generates a unique QR code for your restaurant. If you operate multiple dining areas or floors, you can generate separate QR codes per zone or even per table so orders are tagged correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print and place the QR code on your tables">
    Print the QR code and display it wherever your guests sit — table tents, placemats, tabletop stands, or stickers on the table surface work well. A simple laminated card is enough. You do not need to reprint this code when you update your menu; the code always points to your live Panca catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customers scan to view or order">
    Guests scan the code, browse your menu, and either flag their waiter or tap **Order** to send items directly to the kitchen. You receive the order in your Panca POS in real time, exactly as if a waiter had entered it manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Variants and Add-ons

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sizes" icon="ruler" href="/restaurants/digital-menu">
    Offer items in multiple sizes — small, medium, large, or custom names like "personal" and "familiar." Each size has its own price and Panca tracks inventory separately per size.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extras and Add-ons" icon="circle-plus" href="/restaurants/digital-menu">
    Let customers add extras at ordering time — a shot of espresso, extra cheese, or a sauce on the side. Extras can carry their own additional price or be offered free.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Modifications" icon="pen-to-square" href="/restaurants/digital-menu">
    Customers can flag common modifications like "sin cebolla" or "sin gluten" directly from the menu. These notes attach to the order ticket and appear on the kitchen screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sold-Out Toggle" icon="ban" href="/restaurants/digital-menu">
    If you run out of a dish mid-service, mark it as sold out in Panca with one tap. It disappears from the customer-facing menu immediately — no confusing "sorry, we don't have that today" conversations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Variants are especially valuable for **cafeterías**. A single cappuccino entry can offer sizes (S / M / L), milk types (regular, soy, almond, oat), and shot strength — all selectable from the QR menu. The kitchen and bar receive the full specification on every ticket.
</Note>

## Instant Updates — No Reprinting Required

Unlike a printed menu, your Carta QR reflects your current Panca catalogue at all times. When you need to make a change, you update it in Panca and it is live within seconds:

- **Change a price** → customers scanning the QR code see the new price immediately.
- **Add a seasonal dish** → upload the photo and description and it appears on the menu right away.
- **Remove an item** → deactivate it in Panca and it vanishes from the customer view without any graphic design work.
- **Run a promotion** → apply a temporary discount to any item and it shows on the digital menu for the duration you set.

<Tip>
  High-quality, appetizing photos are one of the most effective tools for increasing your average ticket. Customers who browse a visual menu consistently spend more than those reading a text-only list. Invest in good food photography for your top sellers and most profitable dishes — dishes with photos typically receive two to three times more orders than those without. Update your photos seasonally to keep the menu feeling fresh.
</Tip>
