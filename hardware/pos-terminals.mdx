A 1-hour battery backup keeps you selling through power cuts — one of the most common disruptions in Peruvian restaurant operations.

## Falcon 2 {#falcon-2}

The Falcon 2 is Panca's premium terminal, engineered for restaurants that cannot afford a slow moment at the register. The jump from 2 GB to 4 GB of RAM and the move to Android 11 mean the system stays snappy even when multiple waiters are sending orders simultaneously. The printer runs at 300 mm/s — fast enough that receipts are done before the next customer steps up — and the 2-hour battery backup gives you peace of mind on busy weekends when the power grid is unpredictable. A rear port lets you connect an optional 10" customer-facing display so diners can confirm their order and total in real time.

## Swan 2 {#swan-2}

The Swan 2 solves a specific problem: you need a full POS workstation with an integrated printer, but your counter is too small to accommodate a bulky terminal. Its 15.6" touchscreen is the largest in the lineup, making the interface easy to read at a glance, while the overall unit footprint stays compact thanks to its integrated printer design. The 64 GB SSD and 4 GB of RAM give it solid storage and performance — on par with the Falcon 2 — without the premium price. It doesn't include a battery backup, so it's best suited for locations with stable power or where continuous operation during outages isn't a priority.
