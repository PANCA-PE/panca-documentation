Sometimes you already have a POS terminal or a tablet running Panca, and what you need is a reliable printer for the kitchen, a cash drawer that opens automatically at checkout, or a scanner to speed up retail sales. This page covers every standalone accessory in the Panca hardware catalog — from an entry-level thermal printer to a full self-service kiosk — so you can build exactly the setup your location needs.

<Note>
  All thermal printers in this catalog use standard 80mm paper rolls, which you can restock at any office supply or tech store across Peru. You are never locked into a proprietary consumable.
</Note>

## Thermal printers

### Impresora Térmica 80mm Parpos — S/475

The Parpos is the most popular standalone printer in the Panca catalog. At 300 mm/s with automatic paper cut, it dispatches kitchen tickets and electronic receipts in under two seconds — fast enough to keep pace with a full house at lunch. It connects via USB or Bluetooth out of the box, and an optional LAN module is available if you need a wired network connection. It works with PANCA and with any other system that speaks ESC/POS, so it fits into an existing setup without friction.

### Impresora Térmica 80mm POS-808L — S/475

The POS-808L matches the Parpos on price and print speed, but it ships with USB, LAN, and Bluetooth all enabled by default — no optional modules needed. This makes it the better choice when you need to share one printer across multiple devices on the same network, such as a kitchen station that receives tickets from both a POS terminal and a waiter's tablet.

<Tip>
  If your kitchen has more than one print station — for example, a hot line and a cold line that each need their own printer — the **POS-808L's** built-in LAN and Bluetooth connections let both stations share the same network without extra adapters or configuration work.
</Tip>

It's the natural companion for retail stores, minimarkets, and any restaurant that tracks packaged items by SKU.

## Self-service kiosk

### Kiosco Autoservicio KPOS-6210+ — S/8,000

The KPOS-6210+ is a freestanding self-service kiosk that lets customers browse your menu, place their order, and pay — all without staff involvement. The 21.5" HD touchscreen displays photos of your dishes, which naturally encourages customers to explore add-ons and upsells. It accepts every major Peruvian payment method — Yape, Plin, card, and cash — through integrated readers, so you don't need a separate POS device next to it. Orders flow directly into Panca in real time: the kitchen screen updates instantly and inventory adjusts automatically.

<Note>
