Panca runs in any modern browser, so you can start taking orders on a tablet, phone, or PC you already own — no special equipment required. That said, purpose-built hardware makes a real difference: faster checkouts, reliable receipt printing, and a setup that feels professional to both your staff and your customers. Every product in the Panca hardware catalog ships to any address in Peru, comes with a one-year warranty, and includes free remote configuration with PANCA so you're up and running the same day it arrives.

<Tip>
  If you already have a tablet, phone, or PC, you can activate Panca today and start selling without purchasing any hardware at all. Add dedicated equipment whenever your operation calls for it.
</Tip>
